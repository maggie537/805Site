Hi, I’m Maggie.

I love learning how things work, solving problems and creating things, so working with computers is a perfect fit for me. Through SB Tech Gal, I get to do that every day for homes and small businesses around Santa Barbara, from networks and security to repairs, backups and compliance.

I care a lot about the environment, which is why I’ll try to repair what you have before suggesting anything new. Keeping e-waste out of the landfill matters to me.

Most of all, I love helping people feel safe and secure. I want you to take back technology by understanding your own devices, and I’m happy to answer every question along the way.
